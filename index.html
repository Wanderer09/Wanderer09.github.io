I am an M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Washington, with expected graduation in March 2028. I earned my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and attended UC Berkeley's GLOBE Visiting Program in Spring 2025.

My research experience includes circuit performance prediction, model reliability under distribution shift, and optimization for electronic design automation (EDA). My project work also spans processor design, operating systems, and compiler toolchains. This site brings together the problems I have worked on, my contributions, and the results.
