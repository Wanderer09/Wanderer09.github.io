Co-author · ISEDA 2026

SP-BO: Simulation-Pruning Bayesian Optimization

SP-BO is a simulation-pruning Bayesian optimization framework for analog circuit sizing. It addresses the cost of evaluating candidate designs through circuit simulation.

Search methodBayesian optimization
Evaluation strategySimulation pruning
ApplicationAnalog circuit sizing

I co-authored this collaborative work (ISEDA 2026). It complements my thesis on learning circuit performance from historical simulation data.

Research contribution · BTD Technology

AMSchem: Netlist-to-Schematic Generation

AMSchem is a verifier-guided agentic framework for converting netlists into schematics. It uses structured representations and iterative visual refinement.

  1. NetlistCircuit connectivity as input.
  2. RepresentationStructured circuit information.
  3. SchematicA visual circuit representation.
  4. RefinementVerification guides iterative improvements.

Conceptual overview of the research task.

I contributed to this collaborative framework. Its focus extends my circuit-modeling work to the representation and visual communication of circuit structure.
