BDD Optimization for AI-assisted Processor IP

In this research project, I worked on an AI-assisted processor IP library for digital circuit design.

My work focused on reducing Binary Decision Diagram size through variable-reordering methods that combine classical heuristics with evolutionary and Bayesian search, improving implementation simplicity and PPA-oriented efficiency on standard benchmarks.

Python, optimization, BDD, digital circuit design, search algorithms
