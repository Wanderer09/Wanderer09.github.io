A little more context

This undergraduate thesis grew from my research internship at BTD Technology. I developed the training, evaluation, and inference workflow for SAR-ADC building blocks, and studied out-of-distribution detection and cross-process transfer. The work evaluates offline predictors; a complete automatic circuit-sizing system is a further step.
