SAR-ADC Surrogate Modeling and Bayesian Optimization

This project focuses on replacing expensive simulator-in-the-loop evaluations in SAR-ADC sizing with a surrogate-modeling pipeline.

I designed an uncertainty-aware, offline dataset-driven framework that adaptively chooses between surrogate predictions and circuit simulator calls, improving sample efficiency and robustness under distribution shift.

Python, Bayesian optimization, Bayesian neural networks, analog circuit sizing
